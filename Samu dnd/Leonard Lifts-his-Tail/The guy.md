- Leonard Lifts-his-Tail is a dragonborn around the age of 25, he is a monk and has an ice affinity
- He writes a book in his free time called "[[1st Homily of the Indecisive Changeling|The Anthology of the Tail Lifter]]", he writes parables and homilies in this book, sometimes entirely made up, sometimes based on real stories that happened to him, he writes into this at night before sleeping, the goal of this book is to spread his moral teachings and make the world a better place.
- While in the guard, he didn't get paid enough to afford training equipment, so he lifted his tail to get stronger, his name Lifts-his-tail comes from this, and also a group of guards accused him of being homosexual since the overwhelming friendliness with some people, and not talking to many women, meanwhile this was just because women didn't like him that much while in the guard, the reasons for this are still something he ponders on
- He plays an 8-string acoustic guitar, his late father taught him how to play an acoustic as a child, however he wanted to express his emotions with lower notes, and he understood how the guitar worked very well, so nothing stopped him from adding 2 extra strings to his guitar, he has had this guitar since he was 14.
- Generally a person who tries to be kind, and always improve on himself. But is not fond of most political figures and leaders, also condemns the concept of money.
- He loves spears and uses them since being in the guard, one time he tried dual wielding spears, did not go well, that's how he got a scar on his left eye.
- After realizing the true purpose of a guard is to do orders for people in power, he left the guard and developed a deep disliking for the capital, however he did not begin to hate the capital, since he condemns hate as an emotion, he believes that "We don't have to like everything, but we have to love all we can." - ("The Anthology of the tail lifter", 2nd Homily of the Indecisive Changeling)![[leonardliftshistail.png|650]]
