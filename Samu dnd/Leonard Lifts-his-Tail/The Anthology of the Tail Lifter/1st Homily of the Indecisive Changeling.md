There was once a changeling, who lived in the great Graphite Mountains. It lived with its 2 brothers, they hunted together, they went out to adventure together, they did everything they could together, and they all kept their forms the same, as a green, slime-like being. However, on a sunny day, one of the brothers, "Coryn" started changing his form every day, he never felt right in his skin, he started thinking that being a changeling was a curse.

He didn't understand why he had to suffer like this, never feeling like he belongs anywhere. His brothers didn't like this behavior, but only because they didn't understand why he does this. One day, the brothers decided to go into a mine, since it was rumored to have great treasure inside, they fought off skeletons and goblins one-by-one. Coryn was fighting in a very sloppy manner, it felt like he wasn't trying at all, as if he wanted to lose.

They continued down the mines, and came across a puzzle, a puzzle containing 3 pillars that needed their rotation to be matched with 3 other pillars in the mines, so they decided to split up. Each brother was making their way to the pillars quite smoothly, but Coryn, he froze, he was expecting the brothers to notice that he's sad, that he doesn't feel right in his skin at all, but they just left him like that, alone, to fight in a sloppy form and find the pillar. He cried, he cried until his brothers returned, both worn down from battle, they found Coryn crying on the floor, they asked him what the pillar showed him. Coryn just kept crying, and his brothers were disappointed, but they still wondered, what's wrong with him?
One of the brothers decided to pray to Athena for answers, but instead of Athena, Leonard appeared from a trapdoor and said:

-Are you really so foolish? Have you not even thought of asking? You care for each others needs for survival and such, but when it's a little awkward to help, you just sit there and don't even try? You cannot call yourself a brother if you act like this.

-And you there, Coryn, are you really so foolish? Have you not even thought of speaking about what troubles you? You can act sad and wait for help all you want, but it just ruins the mood of people around you, and leaves you disappointed too. You can rely on your brothers, so rely on them, never be afraid to talk about your troubles, and never sit in despair so **sloth**fully.
